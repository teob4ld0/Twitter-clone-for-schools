# Privacy Policy for Twittetec

**Last Updated:** February 18, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Twittetec**, a social network designed for schools. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our application.

By using Twittetec, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not use the application.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information
- **Username** (maximum 25 characters)
- **Email address** (verified)
- **Password** (stored encrypted as hash)
- **Account creation date**
- **Profile picture** (optional, public URL)
- **Email verification status**
- **Account status** (active/banned)

### 2.2 User-Generated Content
- **Posts (Statuses):** Text, images, and videos you share
- **Replies and comments** to other users' posts
- **Likes** on posts
- **Reposts** of other users' content
- **Direct messages** sent through chat

### 2.3 Social Interaction Data
- **Followers and following:** List of users you follow and who follow you
- **Mutual followers**
- **User profile interactions** (profile visits)
- **Interest signals:** Activity logs such as opening user profiles, interactions with posts

### 2.4 Usage and Analytics Data
- **Timestamps** of activities (post creation, messages, etc.)
- **App usage patterns**
- **Content interaction history**

### 2.5 Google OAuth Authentication (Optional)
If you choose to sign in with Google, we collect:
- Your Google email address
- Your Google username
- Your Google ID

### 2.6 Push Notifications (Optional)
- **Device tokens** to send notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)

### 2.7 Device Permissions (Mobile App)
On mobile devices, we may request the following permissions:
- **Camera access:** To take photos or videos directly from the app for posts or profile pictures
- **Photo/media library access:** To select existing photos and videos from your device to share in posts, messages, or as your profile picture

These permissions are optional and requested only when you perform an action that requires them (e.g., attaching media to a post). Previously, media was uploaded through the file explorer (on PC) or via Google Photos (which already had its own permissions). With the mobile app, direct device access is needed for a better user experience.

You can revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings.

## 4. Sharing Your Information

### 4.1 Public Information
The following information is **public** and visible to all app users:
- Your username
- Your profile picture
- Your posts (unless they are private replies)
- Your follower and following lists
- Your likes and reposts
- Post, follower, and following counts

### 4.2 Private Information
The following data is **private** and not shared:
- Your email address
- Your password (stored as encrypted hash)
- Your direct messages (only visible to you and the recipient)
- Authentication tokens

### 4.3 Third Parties
We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

We share information with service providers:
- **Google Cloud Storage:** To store images and videos (with public URLs)
- **Google OAuth:** If you sign in with Google
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging:** To send push notifications

## 5. Data Storage and Security

### 5.1 Data Location
- Data is stored in a **MySQL database**
- Images and videos are stored on **Google Cloud Storage** or locally on the server
- Authentication data is protected with **JWT tokens**

### 5.2 Security Measures
- Passwords are stored with **cryptographic hash** (never in plain text)
- Communications are protected via **HTTPS**
- Authentication tokens with **automatic expiration**
- Mandatory email verification

### 5.3 Data Retention
- We maintain your data while your account is active
- If you delete your account, your personal data will be deleted (see section 7)
- Public posts may remain stored for archival or legal reasons
